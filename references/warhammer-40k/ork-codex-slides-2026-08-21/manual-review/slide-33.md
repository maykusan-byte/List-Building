Before selecting targets for the Gaze of Gork, select one of its eligible profiles to make attacks with.

Core abilities: Deadly Demise D3, Firing Deck 11. Army rule: Waaagh!

- **Raucous Occupants:** While this model is within range of your home objective, add the OC of models embarked within this model to this model’s OC.
- **Fortification:** While an enemy unit is engaged with only **FORTIFICATION** units:
  - That enemy unit can be selected as a target of ranged attacks.
  - When shooting that enemy unit, those ranged attacks have -1 to Hit rolls (excluding **[CLOSE-QUARTERS]** attacks).
  - When that enemy unit is selected to make a fall-back move, if that enemy unit is only engaged with **FORTIFICATION** units and that enemy unit is not battle-shocked, hazard rolls made for that enemy unit are automatically passed.
- **Ramshackle Cover:** When an attack targets a unit that is not fully visible to the attacking model because of this unit, the target has the benefit of cover against that attack.

Wargear option: This model can be equipped with up to 3 Big Shootas.

Transport: 11 **ORKS INFANTRY** models. It cannot transport **GHAZGHKULL THRAKA** models. Each **MEGA ARMOUR/JUMP PACK** model takes up the space of 2 models.

Unit: 1 Big’ed Bossbunka model, equipped with 1 Big Shoota and 1 Gaze of Gork.  
Keywords: **VEHICLE, FORTIFICATION, FRAME, TRANSPORT**. Faction: **ORKS**.
