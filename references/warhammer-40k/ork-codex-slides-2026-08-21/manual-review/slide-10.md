## Abilities

- **Da Grand Warlord’s Ladz:** While this unit is within 3" of another friendly **ORKS INFANTRY** unit, this unit has Lone Operative.
- **Makari, Hoist Dat Banner! (once per battle, per army):** In your Movement phase, you can select a number of friendly **ORKS** units equal to or less than the current battle-round number. Those units are riled up until the start of your next turn. Place one Makari token next to the unit; remove it after this ability is used.
- **Prophet of da Great Waaagh! (Aura):** While a friendly **ORKS** unit is within 6" of this unit, that unit’s melee attacks have +1 to Hit rolls and +1 to Wound rolls.

## Supreme Commander

If this model is in your army, it must be your **WARLORD**.

## Unit composition

- Equipped with 1 Adamantine Talons, 1 Gork’s Klaw and 1 Mork’s Roar.
