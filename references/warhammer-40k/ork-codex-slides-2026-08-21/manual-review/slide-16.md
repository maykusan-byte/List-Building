Core ability: Support. Army rule: Waaagh!

# Painboss

| 6" | 5 | 4+ | 4 | 7+ | 1 | 6+ |

| Weapon | Keywords | Range | A | BS/WS | S | AP | D |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Beast Snagga Klaw | **[ANTI-MONSTER/VEHICLE 4+]** | Melee | 3 | 3+ | 10 | -2 | 2 |

Core ability: Support. Army rule: Waaagh!

- **Push Dat Bit Back In (once per battle, per unit):** In your Command phase, select one friendly **BEAST SNAGGA** unit within 6" of this unit. That unit heals 3 wounds. Place a Grot Orderly token next to the unit, removing it when this ability is used.
- **Hardy Bioniks:** Attacks that target this unit with a Strength greater than this unit’s Toughness have -1 to Wound rolls.
- **Runnin’ wiv da Herd:** While this model is attached to a **MOUNTED** unit, this model:
  - Does not have **INFANTRY**.
  - Has **MOBILE** and **MOUNTED**.
  - Has 10" Move.

Unit: 1 Painboss model, equipped with 1 Beast Snagga Klaw.  
Keywords: **INFANTRY, BEAST SNAGGA, CHARACTER**. Faction: **ORKS**.
