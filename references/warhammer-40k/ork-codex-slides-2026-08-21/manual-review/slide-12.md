- **Dodge Dis!:** This unit’s attacks have +1 to Hit rolls.

## Ability — Full Throttle

In your Command phase, select one of this unit’s Throttlerokkit Shokka Engine abilities to use.

## Throttlerokkit Shokka Engine abilities

- **Turbo Engine:** When this unit is selected to make an advance/fall-back move, that move does not prevent this unit from being eligible to declare a charge.
- **Shokk Attack Engine:** In your Command phase, if this unit is unengaged, you can place this unit in strategic reserves.
- **Pulse Jet:** In your Movement phase, when this unit is selected to move, if this unit is unengaged, this unit can make a pulse jet move (page 121).

## Unit composition and keywords

- 1 Wazdakka Gutsmek model, equipped with 1 Fixit’s Wrench, 1 Grabba Dragga and 1 Psyko-gattler.
- **MOUNTED, VEHICLE, CHARACTER, EPIC HERO, SMOKE, SPEED FREEKS**. Faction: **ORKS**.
