Army rule: Waaagh!

- **Bomb Squig (once per battle, per unit):** In your Movement phase, when this unit ends a normal move, you can select one visible enemy unit within 12" of this unit and roll one D6. On a 3+, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds. Place one Bomb Squig token next to the unit, removing it when this ability is used.

Unit: 1–2 Nob on Smasha Squig models and 3–6 Squighog Boy models. Every Nob on Smasha Squig is equipped with 1 Big Choppa, 1 Slugga and 1 Squig’s Jaws. Every Squighog Boy is equipped with 1 Saddlegit’s Shiv, 1 Squig’s Jaws and 1 Stikka.
