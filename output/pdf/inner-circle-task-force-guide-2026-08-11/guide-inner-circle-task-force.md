# Guide tactique Dark Angels - Inner Circle Task Force

> Version inner-circle-task-force-guide/v1.0.0 - 2026-08-11 - statut `draft/preliminary`

## Le plan en deux minutes

Inner Circle Task Force n'est pas une simple liste de Terminators. Son vrai moteur consiste a annoncer, au debut de la phase de Mouvement, l'objectif sur lequel la Deathwing doit convertir sa qualite de combat. Defensive Footing protege un acquis; Aggressive Push designe les positions encore adverses. Le detachement devient fort lorsque cette declaration organise tout le tour : entree de reserve, nettoyage, charge, consolidation et releve.

Choisir l'objectif jure, superposer les couches Deathwing et garder une bascule mobile pour le dernier round.

### Variante strictement inventaire

La variante stricte remplace exactement les six Inner Circle Companions par six Bladeguard Veterans. Elle conserve 2 000 points, mais Azrael perd un accompagnement naturellement Deathwing et la liste devient moins fluide autour de l'objectif jure.

## Regle du detachement

**VOWED TARGET** - At the start of your Movement phase, select one of the following:
▪ Defensive Footing: Select one objective you control. Until the start of your next Movement phase, that objective is your Vowed objective.
▪ Aggressive Push: Select one or more objective you do not control. Until the start of your next Movement phase, each of those objectives is one of your Vowed objectives. If a rule refers to a unit or model being within range of your Vowed objective, that rule takes effect if that unit or model is within range of one or more of your Vowed objectives. Each time a Deathwing Infantry unit from your army makes an attack that targets a unit within range of one or more of your Vowed objectives, add 1 to the Wound roll.

## Stratagemes

### Armour of Contempt - 1 PC

**Quand :** Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.  
**Cible :** One Adeptus Astartes unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.  
**Effet :** Until the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, each time an attack targets your unit, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of that attack by 1.  
**Restriction :** Aucune restriction supplementaire structuree.  

### Martial Mastery - 1 PC

**Quand :** The Fight phase.  
**Cible :** One Deathwing Infantry unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.  
**Effet :** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1. If your unit is within range of your Vowed objective marker, you can re-roll the Wound roll instead.  
**Restriction :** Aucune restriction supplementaire structuree.  

### Duty Unto Death - 1 PC

**Quand :** Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.  
**Cible :** One Deathwing unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit's attacks.  
**Effet :** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit is destroyed, if that model has not fought this phase, roll one D6, adding 1 if your unit is within range of your Vowed objective marker. On a 4+, do not remove the destroyed model from play; it can fight after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, and is then removed from play.  
**Restriction :** Aucune restriction supplementaire structuree.  

### Relic Teleportarium - 1 PC

**Quand :** Your Movement phase.  
**Cible :** One Deathwing unit from your army that is arriving using the Deep Strike ability this phase.  
**Effet :** Your unit can be set up anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy models.  
**Restriction :** Until the end of the turn your unit is not eligible to declare a charge.  

### Wrath of the Lion - 1 PC

**Quand :** Your Charge phase.  
**Cible :** One Deathwing Infantry unit from your army that just ended a Charge mode.  
**Effet :** Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of your unit and roll one D6 for each model in your unit, adding 1 to the result if that enemy unit is within range of your Vowed objective marker; for each 4+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound (to a maximum of 3 mortal wounds).  
**Restriction :** Aucune restriction supplementaire structuree.  

### Unmatched Fortitude - 1 PC

**Quand :** Your opponent’s Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.  
**Cible :** One Deathwing Infantry unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking units attacks.  
**Effet :** Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, if the Strength characteristic of that attack is greater than your units Toughness characteristic, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.  
**Restriction :** Aucune restriction supplementaire structuree.

## Doctrine de portee

### Engagement

Melee et Pistol seulement selon l'eligibilite. La Deathwing convertit l'objectif jure; les pistolets restent un appoint.

### 0-9 pouces

Charge, Pistol, Melta a demi-portee. Zone de decision : proteger la consolidation et la releve avant de chercher le dommage maximal.

### 10-12 pouces

Plasma, Torrent eventuel, arrivee a plus de 6 pouces. Relic Teleportarium facilite la position, mais interdit la charge a l'unite ciblee ce tour.

### 13-18 pouces

Rapid Fire selon la demi-portee de l'arme. Les Hellblasters et Terminators preparent l'objectif; verifier la portee propre de chaque profil.

### 19-24 pouces

Plasma et bolters longue portee. Nettoyer sans exposer simultanement les trois couches Deathwing.

### 25-36+ pouces

Lancer et Ballistus. Ouvrir une lane ou retirer le transport qui livrerait le contre-jeu adverse.

## Les cinq confrontations primaires

### Secure Asset contre Take and Hold

Gagner la majorite par vagues : construire deux objectifs solides, puis rendre instable le troisieme objectif adverse.

**Point d'appui :** Un objectif que les Deathwing peuvent rejoindre en deux couches, couvert par un angle de Hellblasters ou de char.

**Premier joueur :** Declarer un objectif jure defendable et montrer une seule couche; garder l'autre hors de la reponse immediate.

**Second joueur :** Laisser l'adversaire reveler son operateur, puis designer l'objectif qui transforme la contre-attaque Deathwing en reprise.

Plan par round :
1. R1 : mesurer les axes, proteger les operateurs et refuser l'exposition simultanee des couches.
2. R2 : engager une seule ressource offensive et conserver une releve a distance de la reponse adverse.
3. R3 : remplacer la premiere couche usee, puis choisir si le centre ou un flanc devient la priorite.
4. R4 : attribuer des maintenant chaque unite survivante au primaire, a une secondaire ou a la bascule finale.
5. R5 : compter l'OC et les positions legales avant les dommages; la derniere unite mobile ne poursuit pas une cible secondaire.

**Condition d'abandon :** Si operateur, releve et soutien peuvent etre touches par la meme reponse, reduire l'ambition et reconstruire la profondeur.

**Repli :** Tenir deux positions, nettoyer l'unite qui produit le double rendement adverse et reporter l'activation de score plutot que l'annoncer acquise.

**Exemple :** Au debut du round 3 de Secure Asset, l'adversaire peut contester l'objectif cle avec une unite ecran et garde sa piece de contre-attaque en retrait. Envoyer la piece la plus puissante maintenant, ou retirer l'ecran avec une couche secondaire avant de basculer ?

- Branche favorable : Si le nettoyage ouvre l'objectif sans exposer la releve, utiliser le tir ou la petite vague, puis conserver la brique principale pour la reprise.
- Branche sure : Si la cible restera contestee ou si le contre-jeu prend deux couches a la fois, tenir la position, ecranter et reporter la bascule.

**A retenir :** L'objectif jure doit organiser le tour; il ne doit jamais devenir une obligation de charger une cible sans valeur de mission.
